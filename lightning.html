Call for Lightning Talks

Share Your Work at BehavXR'26

IEEE ISMAR 2026 · October 6 · 2:00–5:00 PM · Bari, Italy + Hybrid

Already Going to ISMAR 2026?

We’re opening a small number of non-archival lightning talk slots at BehavXR'26 for early-career researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of XR and behavioral health.

Bring an emerging project, highlight an ISMAR poster/demo, or share ongoing work with the community for focused feedback and discussion. No paper is required. Priority may be given to in-person participants.
